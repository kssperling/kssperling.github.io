My contribution

In this team project, I developed the frontend and semantic search. I also contributed to parts of the backend and integration with language models.

What I implemented

  • React/TypeScript frontend for candidate and employer workflows.
  • Embedding-based search for jobs and resumes using PostgreSQL/pgvector.
